# Creative Briefs for Blog Images

## Article 1: Tab Hoarding Isn't Your Fault

### OG Image (`tab-hoarding-og.png`)

**Size:** 1200 x 630px (standard OG image)

**Concept:** Visual representation of "many tabs = context, not chaos"

**Option A: Browser Bar Visualization**
- Show a stylized browser tab bar with many tabs
- Tabs fade from "chaotic" on left to "organized" on right
- Text overlay: "Tab hoarding isn't your fault"
- Subtext: "The problem is retrieval, not organization"
- Daysift logo in corner

**Option B: Minimal Text-Based**
- Clean background using Daysift brand color (#F8F7F4)
- Large headline: "Tab hoarding isn't your fault"
- Small Daysift logo
- Subtle accent (#F46B45) underline or element

**Option C: Conceptual Illustration**
- Simple line illustration of a person at computer
- Many browser tabs shown as thought bubbles
- Not cluttered/negative—more like "active thinking"
- Title text overlaid

**Brand Guidelines:**
- Background: #F8F7F4 (Daysift bg) or white
- Accent: #F46B45 (Daysift orange)
- Font: Inter (or system font that matches)
- Keep minimal, not cluttered
- Should be readable at small sizes (social previews)

**Avoid:**
- Red/warning colors (we're NOT shaming tab hoarders)
- Cluttered, overwhelming visuals
- Stock photo aesthetic
- Competitor logos or references
