Changelog

What we're shipping, release by release.

1.0.24October 2026

New

  • Optional usage stats — Daysift can now share simple usage stats about how the product is used, so we can see what's useful and what to improve. We don't collect what you search for, the content of your browsing history, or the sites you visit. It's on by default, and you can turn it off anytime in Settings → Data.
1.0.23September 2026

New

  • Private AI, now on Proton — Ask AI and page summaries now run on Proton Lumo, based in Europe. Your text isn't used for training, isn't logged, and isn't kept after it answers. Your history stays on your device, as always.
  • Google Workspace filters — Sheets, Slides, and Forms each get their own filter pill instead of all sitting under Docs.
1.0.20March 2026

Improvements

  • Expanded search results — Free users now see 12 results (was 8); Pro users see 30 with "Show more" to load up to 100.
  • Search all your history — Free users can now search all-time history, not just the last 14 days.
  • Faster first experience — History import shows results almost instantly (first 200 items load immediately, the rest in the background).

Fixes

  • Pinned items restored — /pins now shows all your pinned pages, even if they weren't in recent results.
  • Fixed an edge case where the "work" filter could apply by default.
1.0.19March 2026

Performance

  • Faster initial load — results appear instantly when you open Daysift.
  • Parallel data fetching — storage, history cache, and filter rules load simultaneously.
  • Smoother result animations — results fade in with a subtle cascade.

Fixes

  • If your only tab is the Chrome Web Store when you press ⌘J, Daysift now opens a new tab instead of failing silently.
1.0.18March 2026

New

  • Redesigned onboarding — a new welcome flow introduces Search, AI, and Notes with live demos.
  • What's New notifications — when we ship notable features, a bell appears in the footer so you never miss them.
  • Stay in the loop — optional email signup for new features and tips.
  • Smart classification — Daysift decides what to index using heuristics, not just a static allowlist.
  • Mixed-use domain support — YouTube tutorials and Reddit programming threads are indexed; cat videos and memes are not.
  • Domain learning prompts — if you often visit a site that's being excluded, Daysift offers to include it.
  • E / I shortcuts — press E to always exclude a domain, I to always include it.
  • AI streaming — answers appear word-by-word as they're generated.
  • Hybrid shopping detection — e-commerce pages are detected and kept out of your history.

Improvements

  • Skeleton loader on launch — a smooth shimmer while results load, no blank flash.
  • Fixed modal and search-bar height — no more jump or shift as content loads.
  • New asterisk icon for AI, with a subtle spin-and-scale loading animation.
  • Better junk filtering — 404 pages, direct image URLs, and CDN assets no longer clutter history.
  • Domain prompt banner with keyboard navigation (arrow keys to select, Enter to confirm).
  • Fixed Google Groups, Substack, and Spotify favicons; consistent spacing throughout.
  • Search precision — short queries like "AI" no longer match unrelated words like "email".

Fixes

  • Follow-up questions after /sum now work — AI remembers the page content, and streams.
  • "Out of AI credits" now shows the proper upgrade modal everywhere.
  • Onboarding — pressing ⌘J on the final screen now opens Daysift on your previous tab.
1.0.16March 2026

New

  • Page summarization — type /sum to get an AI summary of any page.
  • Floating "Add to Chrome" button as you scroll the landing page.

Improvements

  • Cleaner AI answers — proper section headers, bullet formatting, and spacing.
  • Redesigned AI answer card with copy and regenerate buttons inside the card.
  • Recent results limited to 3 per domain to reduce clutter.
  • Reliable keyboard navigation (↑↓).

Fixes

  • AI consent now appears for page summaries.
  • Arrow keys no longer blocked by the input field; tooltips stay within bounds.
1.0.15February 2026

New

  • Redesigned Settings page with sidebar navigation.
  • Tier-specific upgrade paths and an AI Credits display with a reset countdown.

Improvements

  • Clearer upgrade flow, with a benefits list of what each tier unlocks.
  • Smoother scroll navigation in settings.
1.0.13February 2026

New

  • Open-in-new-tab toggle — choose whether results open in a new tab or replace the current one.

Improvements

  • Refined tooltips and menu hover states; the version number now shows in the Daysift menu.
1.0.10January 2026

New

  • Onboarding flow — new users see a welcome screen on first install.

Fixes

  • Fixed an issue where onboarding wouldn't trigger on fresh installs.
1.0.9January 2026

New

  • Floating notes — capture ideas without leaving your current page.
  • Pinned pages — keep your most-used pages one keystroke away.
  • Domain filters — narrow search results by app.

Improvements

  • Faster search with improved fuzzy matching, and better keyboard navigation throughout.
1.0December 2025

Initial release

  • Command palette search (⌘J on Mac, Alt+J on Windows).
  • Smart history indexing for work apps only.
  • Light and dark themes with system-preference support.
  • Slash commands for quick actions.
